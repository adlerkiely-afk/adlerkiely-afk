## 2027 IREC payload: pitot deployment mechanism
**Kent State Rocket Team · 2026 – present · being built**

![CAD animation of the payload deploying its pitot probes](images/payload1.gif)

One unit of the team's CubeSat-format weather station payload. A single servo drives a symmetrical linkage that pushes the pitot probes out of the payload during descent.

- **Change of plan:** the probes were first meant to measure wind speed. We determined that reading could not be accurate, so the design was re-approached to measure descent speed and give the weather station more data against altitude.
- **Work so far:** mechanism trade study (rack-and-pinion against a slider-crank), stroke and torque sizing, and a full assembly modeled in Onshape.
- **Status:** still being built. It launches at IREC in summer 2027, so there is no flight data yet.

## SpeedUAS: high-speed quadcopter
**Independent project · 2026 – present · in development**

![Wireframe CAD view of the SpeedUAS airframe](images/wiref.png)

A 5-inch, 6S quadcopter with a streamlined body-of-revolution fuselage, modeled in SolidWorks.

- **Work so far:** airframe CAD, PLA fit-check prints, and corrections to the model from caliper measurements.
- **In progress:** CFD setup and meshing in Ansys Fluent. Nothing is solved yet.
- **Status:** not assembled, not flown.

Repository: [SpeedUAS](https://github.com/adlerkiely-afk/SpeedUAS)
